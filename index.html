About Me

I am an applied economist with extensive experience in research, policy analysis, and academic teaching. My work focuses on delivering evidence-based insights that inform effective economic policies and business strategies.

With a strong quantitative background and practical field experience, I specialize in transforming complex economic data into actionable recommendations for policymakers, businesses, and international organizations.

My research approach combines rigorous econometric analysis with deep contextual understanding to address real-world economic challenges in developing and emerging markets.
